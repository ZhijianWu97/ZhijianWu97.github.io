Zhijian Wu

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the Medical Artificial Intelligence Lab, Westlake University.

Research

My research focuses on building generalizable and efficient AI systems for real-world visual intelligence and biomedical discovery. Specifically, I study how to develop models and training paradigms that can robustly generalize across tasks, domains, and data distributions, while remaining computationally and memory efficient. My recent interests include efficient image restoration and enhancement, token compression for video LLMs, acceleration of diffusion-based LLMs, and generalizable AI for medical imaging and biology. My long-term goal is to build practical AI systems that are not only powerful and scalable, but also useful for real-world deployment and scientific discovery.
